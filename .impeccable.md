# LoreStudio — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Writers ranging from serious novelists (long sessions, complex projects, multi-book organization) to hobbyist worldbuilders (creative exploration, a few hours a week). Both audiences share a need for focus and clarity, but differ in how much structural complexity they want visible at once. The tool must feel equally accessible to a first-timer and indispensable to a seasoned author.

### Brand Personality
Professional studio — focused, refined, craft-forward. The kind of tool a serious author would pay for and feel proud to use. Clean, trustworthy, no-nonsense. Should feel purpose-built for writing, not a generic productivity wrapper. 

Three words: **Intentional. Refined. Alive.**

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Warm and typographic**: The parchment-toned palette (`#f7f6f3` base, forest green accent, warm text) is the right instinct and is the clearest piece of visual identity. Lean into this further rather than letting generic patterns dilute it.
- **No strong reference app**: LoreStudio should feel like its own thing — distinctive among writing tools. Not Notion, not Scrivener, not iA Writer.
- **Anti-reference**: Generic SaaS dashboards, AI-tool aesthetics (dark mode + glowing purple/cyan), "feature-forward" UIs that look like product screenshots.
- **Themes**: Light Zen (primary), Dark Zen, E-ink variants. Light is the canonical design direction; dark should feel like an intentional inversion, not the default.

### Design Principles
1. **Warm restraint**: The palette is warm and distinctive — let it breathe. Resist adding visual complexity that fights the base aesthetic.
2. **Progressive revelation**: LoreStudio has ~15+ features in the sidebar. Not everything is needed by everyone. Surface the right things at the right moment.
3. **Typography carries the weight**: This is a writing tool. Type hierarchy and typographic rhythm should do most of the design work.
4. **Color as signal, not decoration**: The existing color system (segment colors, AI purple, NLP teal, editorial green) is semantically rich. Ensure each use is earned and consistent.
5. **Studio-grade polish**: Details matter — spacing rhythm, icon alignment, hover states, empty states. Small roughness destroys the "professional studio" feeling faster than any structural issue.
