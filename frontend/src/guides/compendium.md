# The Compendium

Your research and reference: what you read, saved, sketched and noted while making the story. It informs the story without being part of it.

Open logo menu › **Compendium** and pick a section: **Everything**, **Research**, **Notes**, **Images** or **Diagrams**.

## The Compendium and the Lorebook

The Lorebook is what's true in your story. The Compendium is what's true in the world you borrowed from.

Eleanor Vance and Harrow Island belong in the Lorebook: they exist because you made them up. What a real lighthouse keeper's duties were, or how isolation wears on a person, belongs in the Compendium: it was true before your story, and you are drawing on it. _The Last Lighthouse_ keeps both of those as research: _Lighthouse Keeper Duties & History_ and _Isolation & Psychological Effects_.

## Everything

One list of the whole Compendium, newest first: research, images and diagrams together, for when you can't remember where you put something.

- **Search the Compendium…** finds an entry by its words.
- The chips (**Notes**, **Links**, **Documents**, **Images**, **Diagrams**) narrow it to one kind.
- **Add research** starts a new entry.

## Research

Your research entries, each one of three kinds:

- **Note**: research in your own words.
- **URL**: a link to a page. **Auto-fetch title and description** fills those in for you.
- **Document**: a file, such as a PDF, uploaded in **Images** first and linked here.

**Add entry** makes one. Each has a **Category** and **Tags** to find it by, and **Notes** for why it matters to your story. **Search research…** and the filters along the top narrow the list.

## Notes

Every note, question, to-do and idea in the story, in story order, wherever it is tied: a scene, a character, a place, or nothing yet. See [Notes, Freewrite and the scratch pad](/guides/notes-and-freewrite).

## Images

Drop images or documents onto the page, or choose **Upload**. Open an image for its sheet:

- **What it shows**: a caption, which screen readers read too.
- **Your note**: why it is here and what it is for.
- **Used by**: the character, place or scene that uses it, each a link. A character or place can take any image as its picture.
- **Replace file…** swaps in a new file and keeps the caption, the note and every use.

## Diagrams

Mindmaps and flowcharts for working things out. **New diagram** starts one. An empty diagram offers to start from a template, such as **Character relationship web**, **Plot timeline** or **Mystery clue map**.

## In a series

In a book of a series, a research entry or an image can be shared with the other books: **Share with the series** on its sheet. An edit to a shared one is every book's. See [Writing a series](/guides/writing-a-series).
