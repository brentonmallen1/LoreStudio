# Glossary

What the words in LoreStudio mean, grouped by where you meet them.

## The parts of a story

- **Chronicle.** The story's history: snapshots, every change you made (with undo) and what ran in the background.
- **Compendium.** Your research and reference: documents, links, notes, images and diagrams. It informs the story without being part of it.
- **Findings.** What the checks noticed and that may need your eye, such as an unknown speaker or a slip in point of view. Each is a question, not a verdict.
- **Lorebook.** Everything true in your story: its identity and intent, characters, places and how the world works.
- **Manuscript.** The prose itself, and the page where you read the book straight through and export it.
- **Plan.** The page where you work out the story before or while you write it, from an idea in a sentence to a list of scenes.
- **Promises.** What the story promises the reader and where it keeps them: threads, twists, setups and payoffs, and what the reader knows.
- **Proposals.** Things LoreStudio found in your prose and has not added on its own, such as a new name or a line with no speaker. You accept or dismiss each one.
- **Story Identity.** The first page of the Lorebook: the logline, premise, themes, goals and your byline.

## Writing

- **Freewrite.** A page for loose writing, a heading for each day, with no structure to keep to. Turn what you find there into notes or characters.
- **Mention.** A name in your prose linked to the Lorebook: `@Name` for a character, `[[Name]]` for a place.
- **Note.** Something to come back to: a note, a question, a to-do or an idea. It can sit in the margin beside the words it is about.
- **Scene sheet.** Every field of one scene on a page: what happens, why, who, where, when, and its promises, notes and findings.
- **Scratch pad.** A page that belongs to no story, kept with your account, for a thought that has nowhere to go yet.
- **Side panel.** The column on the right of the writing page, with tools such as **This scene**, **Characters** and **Notes**.
- **Speaker tag.** A name in angle brackets after a line of dialogue, `<Maya>`, saying who speaks it.
- **Status.** Where a scene is: **Planned**, **Draft**, **Revised** or **Final**.
- **Story strip.** The book's scenes down the left of the page, as a line or the full outline.
- **Structure template.** The named levels of a story's outline, such as Act, Chapter and Scene.
- **This scene.** The scene at a glance, in the side panel.
- **Writer and Studio.** The two modes. Writer keeps to your story; Studio adds more tools.

## Promises

- **Clue.** A detail planted for a twist, pointing toward the truth or away from it.
- **Setup and payoff.** Something shown early so that a later moment lands.
- **Thread.** Something the story opens and later closes: a question, a place, a change in someone or a disruption to their world.
- **Twist.** A truth hidden behind what the reader is led to believe, with clues pointing either way.
- **What the reader knows.** What the reader has learned, and believes, at each point in the book.

## Series

- **Axis.** What changes from book to book, such as whose eyes the story is seen through, an era or a place.
- **Canon.** What the books of a series share: the same characters, places and threads, with what stays the same and what changes from book to book.
- **Coming in.** What the reader knows and believes at the start of a book, from the books before it.
- **Planned book.** A book of the series you have planned but not yet written.
- **Series.** Books that belong together, in reading order.
- **The story so far.** A card for each book of a series saying what it leaves the reader with, as a reminder before the next.

## Keeping your work safe

- **Snapshot.** A copy of the whole story at one moment, which you can restore, compare or download. Find them in Chronicle › **Versions**.
- **Undo.** {{key:undo}} takes back your last change, whether you typed it or changed something on another page.
