# The Chronicle

The story's history: what ran in the background, every change you made, the versions you saved and what each import brought. Come here to find out what happened, and to take something back.

## Open it

Logo menu › **Chronicle**. Its sections are in the logo menu beside it and in the crumbs at the top of the page. Whatever you open in a section shows in a column beside the list, and the address keeps it, so **Back** ({{key:back}}) closes it again.

## Activity

**Activity** is the story's timeline, grouped by day, newest first: every check and analysis that ran, and every job, such as a backup of the story. A job is one row, however many steps it took. Open a row to see what it did and what it was asked.

- **Everything**, **Running and queued**, **Problems** (what failed or was stopped), **Analyses** and **Starred** narrow the list. Under **Analyses**, pick one check to see only its runs.
- Star a row to keep it under **Starred**.
- **Search activity…** finds a row by its words.
- A job still running can be stopped from its row. See [Jobs: work that runs while you write](/guides/jobs).

Every run of a check is kept here in full, so a finding can always show where it came from.

In Studio, Activity also lists every call to the Assistant: open one to see exactly what was sent to the model and what came back. **Results** narrows it to the summaries, analyses and brainstorms worth keeping.

## Conversations

**Conversations** keeps your talks with the Assistant: the scene assistant, character interviews and group panels. Open one to read it back, resume it in the side panel, archive it or delete it. **Search conversations…** finds one by its words. This section is in Studio only.

## Changes

**Changes** lists every change to the story's data, newest first: a renamed character, a moved chapter, a fixed finding, a yes or a no in Discoveries. Each row says what changed, when, and whether it came from this tab or another.

- **Undo** on a row takes back that one change, as long as nothing later depends on it.
- **Undo latest** and **Redo** step back and forward through the newest changes, from any tab.
- Typing shows as a row too, marked **typing, undone in the scene**: take it back in the scene with {{key:undo}}.
- **Load older** goes further back.

In _The Last Lighthouse_, rename Thomas Vance on his sheet and the rename is the top row here, with its **Undo**.

## Versions

**Versions** holds snapshots of the whole story: save one, compare it with now, restore it or download it. See [Snapshots, backups and undo](/guides/snapshots-and-backups).

## Imports

**Imports** keeps a report for each import into this story: what came over, what still waits for you in Discoveries, what had no place in LoreStudio, and the original file to download. See [Import and export](/guides/import-and-export).
