# Search and replace

Find words in a scene or across the whole story, change them safely, and keep names linked to your Lorebook.

## Find in this scene

Press {{key:find}} while you write. Type what you're looking for; **Enter** goes to the next match and **Shift+Enter** to the one before.

- **Match case** and **Whole word** narrow the search.
- Open the replace row to change one match (**Replace**) or every match (**Replace all**).

## Search the whole story

Press {{key:storySearch}}. Every scene with a match is listed with an excerpt; click one to go there.

To change the words everywhere, type the new words in **Replace with…**. Choose **Replace all**, or tick the scenes you want and choose **Replace in selected**. One {{key:undo}} takes back the whole replace.

> **Tip:** The story search also finds your words inside longer ones: "Ann" matches "Anna". Tick only the scenes you mean before you replace.

Search and replace only ever touch your words. A speaker tag such as `<Maya>`, the `@` of a mention and the `[[ ]]` around a place are left alone, so a replace can't break who is speaking or what a name points to.

## Mention a character or a place

Type `@` and a name to mention a character, or `[[` to mention a place. A list of names opens as you type; **Tab** or **Enter** takes one. A mention links your words to the Lorebook entry, so the scene knows who and where it is about.

- A mention can use any of an entry's other names, listed under **Also called** on its sheet. Add one from the sheet's ⋯ menu › **Add another name…**.
- **Link mentions**, in the scene's ⋯ menu, finds names you wrote plainly and offers to link them. Your words stay as they are.

## Fix a mention that names nobody

A mention that matches no one in the Lorebook is underlined in the warning colour. Point at it to fix it:

- **Same as…**: pick who or where it means. The words become one of their other names; the prose is not changed.
- **Add as a character** or **Add to Places**: create a new entry under those words.
- **Unlink**: keep the words and drop the link. {{key:undo}} brings it back.

## Rename a character or a place

Change the name on their sheet (for a character, the sheet's ⋯ menu › **Edit name, role, pronouns…**). While your prose still uses the old name, it is kept under **Also called**, so no mention stops working. Remove it there once you no longer need it.
