# Notes, TODOs and the scratch pad

## Inline notes

Select text in the editor and press **⌘⇧N** (or *Note* in the selection toolbar). The passage is marked; the note lives in the Notes panel and opens on click. Editorial notes created by an AI editorial pass (Studio) show as diamonds and can be hidden with one toggle.

## TODOs

Type `/todo` in the editor to drop a TODO at the cursor; it appears as a gutter marker and in the **TODOs** view of the structure panel, where you can sort, complete and reorder them. TODOs are undoable like everything else.

## Scratch pad

**⌘⇧P** opens a drawer for thoughts that belong nowhere yet. It has a per-story tab and a global tab, saves as you type, and never appears in exports.

## Purpose vs. notes

*Purpose* is what a segment is for; inline notes are what you want to remember about specific passages. Revision goes faster when purpose is filled in first.
