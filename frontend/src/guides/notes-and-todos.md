# Notes, TODOs and the scratch pad

## Inline notes

Select text in the editor and press **{{key:inlineNote}}** (or _Note_ in the selection toolbar). The passage is marked and the note sits in the margin to the left of the prose, level with the words it is about, like a comment in a document. Click the words or the note to open it, edit it or delete it; Esc closes it. When the window is too narrow for the margin, each note is a dot beside its line, and opens just under the words. **Notes** in the top bar shows or hides the margin, and the side panel's _This scene_ tab lists every note in the scene. Editorial notes left by an editorial pass (Studio) are marked in teal and can be hidden with one toggle.

## TODOs

Type `/todo` in the editor to drop a TODO at the cursor; it appears as a gutter marker and in the **TODOs** view of the structure panel, where you can sort, complete and reorder them. TODOs are undoable like everything else.

## Scratch pad

**{{key:scratchPad}}** opens a drawer for thoughts that belong nowhere yet. It has a per-story tab and a global tab, saves as you type, and never appears in exports.

## Purpose vs. notes

_Purpose_ is what a segment is for; inline notes are what you want to remember about specific passages. Revision goes faster when purpose is filled in first.
