# Dialogue and quotes

LoreStudio keeps track of who says what, without changing how you write.

## Attributing a line

Three ways, all producing `"…"<Name>` in the prose:

1. Type `^` and pick the speaker: an empty quoted line appears with the cursor inside.
2. Select spoken text and press **⌘⇧D** (or *Attribute* in the selection toolbar).
3. After a closing quote type `<` and the speaker's name; the picker completes it.

Unattributed quotes are still detected. The **Dialogue** view (top bar) shows every line as a bubble, marks inferred and unattributed speakers, and lets you fix them in bulk with *Tag Suggestions* (heuristic, no AI).

## Quote style

Straight `"…"` and curly `“…”` quotes mixed in one manuscript is the most common copy-edit note. The **Quote style** field in the Notes panel counts both across the story and converts everything with one click, after showing you how many characters change. Speaker tags and markup are never touched. The same command is in the palette: *Normalize quotes*.

## Voice

**Story Health › Dialogue** compares speakers: line length, favourite words, questions, contractions. Distinct characters should read distinctly; the report says when two blur together.

## Checks

The **Checks** field flags dialogue attributed to someone who is not in the Lorebook (a typo, or a character you have not added yet).
