# The story in numbers

See your book measured as it stands, and how it has changed.

## What the page shows

Open logo menu › **Numbers**. Its sections cover length, pacing, threads, who is on the page, whose eyes, who speaks, and prose habits.

Nothing here is a verdict; **Findings** is where something is raised for your eye. The ⓘ beside each section says what it counts and how to read it.

## The numbers keep a history

The page saves its figures now and then as a **reading**:

- when you open the story after three hours away, and at most once more a day;
- with every version you save (⋯ › **Save a version…**);
- whenever you choose ⋯ › **Measure now**.

Readings are measured on your own machine and never make you wait.

## Compare with an earlier reading

1. Choose **Compare** at the top of the page.
2. Pick **This session**, **Last session**, **A week ago**, **A month ago** or a version by name.
3. To compare two earlier readings, choose the second under **Against**.

**What changed** sums it up, and each moved figure says what it **was**. The ✕ stops comparing. **Over time** draws the headline figures across every reading.

> **Tip:** A change is never marked good or bad. More words is not better; the page only says what moved.
