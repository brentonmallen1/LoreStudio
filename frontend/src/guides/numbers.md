# The story in numbers

**Numbers** measures the book as it stands: how long it is and how far along, how much room each scene takes, where each thread runs, who is on the page, who speaks and how much, and the prose's habits. Nothing on the page is a verdict or a suggestion; **Findings** is where something is raised for your eye. The ⓘ beside each section says what it counts, why it is there and how to read it.

## The numbers over time

The page keeps a history of itself. A **reading** is its figures at one moment, a few KB, kept on their own:

- when you open the story after three hours away (the start of a session), and once more in a day if the book changed;
- with every **version** you save, under its name (⋯ › **Save a version…**, or Chronicle › Versions);
- after a restore, which never rewinds the history;
- whenever you choose ⋯ › **Measure now**.

Each reading is a job of its own (see **Jobs: work that runs while you write**), measured on this machine as soon as it is asked for, so saving and walking away never waits; the automatic ones are quiet. Earlier versions are measured the first time the page sees them, as a job too. Every reading is kept for two weeks, then one a day for ninety days, then one a week; a version's is never thinned. Readings travel in a story's export.

## Comparing

**Compare ▾** in the header starts from your latest version, or the reading before now if you have none. Choose **This session**, **Last session**, **A week ago**, **A month ago**, a version by name or any reading; **Against** compares two readings instead of a reading with now. The bar under the header says what is compared and how far apart; ✕ stops comparing. The choice is in the address, so a link opens on it, and the page remembers it per story.

While comparing:

- **What changed** says it in a few sentences;
- the earlier side is drawn quieter than now: outlines for earlier lengths and shares, a ring on what is new, faint marks for what has gone, "was" beside a figure that moved;
- Over time, at the top of the page, draws five headline figures across every reading on one time axis, with each version as a dashed rule; point at the lines to read a reading, choose it to compare with it.

A change is never coloured as good or bad. More words is not better; the page states what moved and by how much.
